# Course Domain

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Author: Project Academy Team

---

# Purpose

A Course represents an educational program offered by an academy.

Every academy may organize its curriculum differently.

Project Academy must adapt to the academy's methodology instead of forcing a predefined structure.

---

# Philosophy

Project Academy follows the academy.

The academy does not follow Project Academy.

Teachers decide how students progress.

The platform supports their teaching process.

---

# Course Structure

A course is composed of Learning Units.

Example

Course

↓

Learning Units

↓

Activities

↓

Mini Games

↓

Assessments

↓

Rewards

The number of Learning Units is not fixed.

Every academy decides its curriculum.

# Unlock System

Courses never unlock automatically.

Teachers decide when a Learning Unit becomes available.

Students cannot skip content.

Learning follows the classroom.

---

# Course Progress

Students progress according to

Teacher evaluation

Attendance

Learning evidence

Academies may define their own progression rules.

# Design Principles

1. Every academy controls its curriculum.

2. Courses are flexible.

3. Learning Units are reusable.

4. Teachers control progression.

5. Students cannot rush the course.

6. Practice is always available.

7. The Game Engine supports every course.

8. The platform adapts to the academy.

9. Learning remains teacher-guided.

10. Gamification increases engagement.
