# Vision

Project Academy is not an English learning application.

Project Academy is a gamified learning platform.

Learning is the purpose.

Fun is the motivation.

Competition is the fuel.

Teachers are the guides.

Students are the heroes.

---

# Mission

Create the most engaging educational platform ever built.

Students should open the application because they WANT to.

Not because they HAVE to.

---

# Product Pillars

Everything inside Project Academy must respect these pillars.

---

## 1. Learning Comes First

Every mechanic must reinforce learning.

Never distract from it.

---

## 2. Fun Drives Motivation

Mini-games exist to increase motivation.

Never replace teaching.

---

## 3. Teachers Stay In Control

Teachers decide the learning pace.

Teachers unlock content.

Teachers evaluate progress.

Teachers remain essential.

---

## 4. Every Academy Is Unique

Every academy has:

Own logo

Own mascot

Own colors

Own events

Own identity

Students should feel

"This is MY academy."

---

## 5. Progress Must Be Visible

Students should always see progress.

XP

Levels

Badges

Inventory

Achievements

Rank

Collections

Everything should communicate progress.

---

## 6. Competition Must Be Healthy

Competition exists to motivate.

Never humiliate.

Never punish.

Everyone should feel capable of improving.

---

## 7. Everything Is Earned

No Pay To Win.

No purchased XP.

No purchased levels.

No purchased exclusive rewards.

Effort creates value.

---

## 8. The Journey Never Ends

Even after completing a course

Students continue collecting

Achievements

Cosmetics

Titles

Pets

Seasonal rewards

Knowledge

---

# The Student Fantasy

The student should never feel

"I'm studying."

The student should feel

"I'm becoming stronger."

Learning English is simply how they become stronger.

---

# The Teacher Fantasy

Teachers should never feel

"I have more work."

Teachers should feel

"I have better tools."

---

# Academy Fantasy

Academies should never feel

"We bought software."

Academies should feel

"We have our own educational game."

---

# Long Term Vision

Project Academy should become

The Steam of educational games.

A platform where different educational experiences can coexist while sharing the same core systems.

---

# Final Principle

If a feature improves learning and makes students excited to return tomorrow,

it belongs in Project Academy.

If it only increases complexity,

it does not.
