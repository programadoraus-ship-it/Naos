# Player Journey

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines the complete emotional journey of a student inside Project Academy.

Project Academy is not designed to make students study.

It is designed to make students WANT to return every day.

Learning is the objective.

Motivation is the vehicle.

---

# Daily Loop

Student opens the app

↓

Receives welcome from academy mascot

↓

Sees today's missions

↓

Claims daily reward

↓

Sees leaderboard

↓

Continues current Learning Unit

↓

Plays mini-games

↓

Earns XP

↓

Unlocks achievements

↓

Checks inventory

↓

Customizes avatar

↓

Leaves wanting to come back tomorrow.

---

# Weekly Loop

Attend classes

↓

Teacher unlocks new Learning Unit

↓

New mini-games become available

↓

Complete weekly missions

↓

Compete in weekly ranking

↓

Receive rewards

↓

Prepare for next week

---

# Monthly Loop

Complete Learning Units

↓

Participate in seasonal events

↓

Compete for Student of the Month

↓

Unlock exclusive cosmetics

↓

Receive certificates

↓

Start a new season

---

# Emotional Goals

Students should feel:

Curiosity

Progress

Competition

Achievement

Belonging

Pride

Improvement

Never frustration.

---

# Motivation Sources

Learning

Competition

Collection

Customization

Achievements

Daily streaks

Seasonal events

Exploration

---

# Long-Term Goals

Become top student.

Complete every Learning Unit.

Collect every badge.

Unlock exclusive cosmetics.

Improve pronunciation.

Reach the highest academy rank.

Become Student of the Month.

---

# Design Principles

Learning should never feel like homework.

Progress should always be visible.

Competition should be healthy.

Teachers remain essential.

Rewards celebrate effort.

Every session should end with a reason to return tomorrow.
