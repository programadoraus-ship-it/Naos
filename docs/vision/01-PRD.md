# 1. Executive Summary

Project Academy is a SaaS platform designed for language schools that transforms learning into a game-like experience.

Instead of replacing teachers, the platform enhances classroom learning through gamification, artificial intelligence, rankings, rewards and student engagement.

The primary customer is the language school.

Students use the application as part of their learning experience.

---

# 2. Vision

To become the world's leading gamified learning platform for language schools.

The platform should motivate students to learn every day while providing schools with modern tools to manage courses, teachers and student progress.

---

# 3. Mission

To increase student motivation and learning outcomes by combining traditional classroom education with game mechanics and artificial intelligence.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Many language schools still manage their students using spreadsheets and manual processes.

Common problems include:

- Low student motivation
- Poor engagement outside the classroom
- Manual attendance tracking
- Limited digital learning resources
- No reward system
- No competition between students
- Lack of learning analytics

---

# 5. Proposed Solution

Project Academy provides a complete ecosystem where students earn rewards by learning.

Students can:

- Complete lessons
- Earn XP
- Earn coins
- Unlock avatars
- Unlock skins
- Complete daily missions
- Participate in weekly events
- Climb leaderboards
- Improve pronunciation
- Practice with AI

Teachers can:

- Record attendance
- Create quizzes
- Assign homework
- Monitor progress

Administrators can:

- Manage students
- Manage teachers
- Assign courses
- Review statistics

# 7. User Roles

## Super Admin

Platform owner.

Responsibilities:

- Create schools
- Manage subscriptions
- Monitor the entire platform

---

## School Administrator

Responsible for one school.

Permissions:

- Accept students
- Create teachers
- Assign courses
- Assign levels
- View reports
- Manage school settings

---

## Teacher

Responsibilities:

- Register attendance
- Create quizzes
- Grade students
- Award bonus points
- Monitor progress

---

## Lesson Designer

Responsibilities:

- Create lessons
- Create activities
- Create exams
- Publish content

---

## Student

Responsibilities:

- Complete lessons
- Earn rewards
- Attend classes
- Compete in rankings
- Customize profile
- Track progress

---

# 8. Core Principles

The platform follows these principles:

- Learning must feel like playing a video game.
- Teachers are never replaced by AI.
- Students cannot buy advantages using real money.
- Rewards must always be earned.
- Healthy competition is encouraged.
- Every school operates independently.
- The platform must support multiple schools.

---

# 9. Business Model

Project Academy is sold as a monthly or annual subscription.

The customer is the language school.

Students do not pay separately.

Revenue comes from schools subscribing to the platform.
