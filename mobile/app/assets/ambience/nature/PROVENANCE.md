# Nature preview artwork

The seven PNGs in this directory were generated specifically for the NAOS Nature preview with OpenAI image generation on 20 September 2026. They are original generated assets, not downloaded third-party artwork. No external attribution, paid resource, or runtime download is required.

- `valley.png`: stationary shared mountain-valley landscape.
- `oak.png`, `summit.png`, `waterfall.png`: transparent scene protagonists for Ancient Grove, Alpine Vista and Waterfall Haven.
- `songbirds.png`, `wildflowers.png`, `leaves.png`: transparent foreground habitat details.

Prompt set: sophisticated painterly 3D-rendered natural storybook illustration, warm morning light, detailed botanicals and landscape depth; isolate movable foreground subjects with genuine transparency, no text or logos. Flutter composites the stationary background and independently editable foreground layers.
