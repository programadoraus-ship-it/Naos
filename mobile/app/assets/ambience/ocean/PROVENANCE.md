# Ocean ambience artwork

These ten original images were generated for NAOS with OpenAI's built-in
image-generation tool on 19–20 September 2026. They were not downloaded from a
third-party asset library and require no third-party attribution. The artwork
is prerendered 2D raster imagery with volumetric/painterly styling; NAOS does
not run a 3D engine for this ambience.

- `seascape.png`: deep underwater background, textured reef edges and light shafts.
- `turtle.png`: transparent sea turtle and companion fish layer.
- `coral.png`: transparent coral and reef fish foreground layer.
- `thumbnail.png`: composition of the three layers for the selection card.
- `shark.png`: transparent friendly shark and companion fish.
- `shark-coral.png`: transparent alternative reef foreground, anchored right.
- `jellyfish.png`: transparent group of three jellyfish and reef fish.
- `fish-school.png`: separately movable group of five reef fish, generated on
  20 September 2026 with the turtle and coral artwork as style references.
- `kelp.png`: separately movable vegetation cluster, generated on 20 September
  2026 with the coral artwork as a style reference.
- `coral-cluster.png`: compact, separately movable coral cluster, generated on
  20 September 2026 with the larger coral artwork as a style reference.

Generation briefs: a wide underwater scene with negative space for UI; a
friendly, textured sea turtle isolated on transparency; a colorful coral reef
cluster anchored to a transparent corner; and a representative composition of
those three images without lettering. No external source images were used.
The three later layers were generated on 19 September 2026 using the existing
NAOS turtle/coral artwork and shared seascape as style and lighting references:
a friendly blue-grey shark with companion fish isolated on transparency; a
distinct brightly coloured reef formation anchored to the right on
transparency; and three luminous, gently drifting jellyfish with fish isolated
on transparency. Each uses coherent underwater light and textured detail,
without baked-in background or lettering.
The three editable layers generated on 20 September use transparent
backgrounds and match the existing turtle/coral palette: a compact school of
five fish, a tall kelp cluster, and a compact coral cluster. They are separate
images so they can be moved and faded independently in the local preview.
All three variants reuse the unchanged `seascape.png` background.
The turtle and its companion fish remain one uncut illustration. In the
preview, Flutter moves and tilts each animal illustration slightly as a whole;
kelp and coral sway subtly. It does not animate individual flippers or eyes.
All ambient movement stops when the system requests reduced motion.
